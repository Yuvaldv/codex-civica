Reading Guide

England-applicable Acts of Parliament — source: legislation.gov.uk, the official UK legislation database, reused under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

How to read a law on this site​

Every Act is converted directly from legislation.gov.uk's own structured XML — no OCR, no AI reconciliation. What you see here mirrors the source exactly.

Not-yet-applied changes​

Some Acts have amendments the publisher has recorded but not yet incorporated into the text. These appear as a banner at the top of the Act:

2 change(s) not yet applied to this Act. The source publisher (legislation.gov.uk) has recorded amendments that are not yet incorporated into the text below:

  • section-14-1: words inserted (by Courts Act 2003)

Repealed and prospective provisions​

Repealed text is kept, never deleted, and clearly marked:

(a) [REPEALED]​

Provisions not yet in force are marked the same way:

(b) [NOT YET IN FORCE — prospective]​

Amendments in the text​

Words inserted, substituted, or repealed by a later Act are shown in brackets with a footnote citing the amending instrument:

he causes a computer to perform any function [, or to enable any such access to be secured]1

Quoted text from another Act​

Text an Act quotes from elsewhere (a BlockAmendment) is shown as a quote block, kept out of this document's own numbering:

[Quoted block text — not this Act's own numbered provision]

the quoted words themselves

Territorial extent​

Where a provision applies to a narrower part of the UK than the Act as a whole, that is noted directly above it:

[Extent — section 3] E+W

Defined terms and editorial notes​

Each Act ends with an alphabetical index of its defined terms, and — where the publisher recorded a note that isn't tied to a specific amendment — an Editorial Notes section.

Attribution​

Every page carries the Open Government Licence v3.0 attribution required for reuse of legislation.gov.uk content, at the foot of the Act.

Footnotes​
