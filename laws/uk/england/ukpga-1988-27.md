Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

# 1. Offence of sending letters <span id="section-1" />

> [Extent — 1. Offence of sending letters] E+W

## (1) <span id="section-1-1" />

Any person who sends to another person—

### (a) <span id="section-1-1-a" />

a [letter, electronic communication or article of any description][^fc803482] which conveys—

#### (i) <span id="section-1-1-a-i" />

a message which is indecent or grossly offensive;

#### (ii) <span id="section-1-1-a-ii" />

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

#### (iii) <span id="section-1-1-a-iii" />

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### (b) <span id="section-1-1-b" />

any [article or electronic communication][^fc803483] which is, in whole or part, of an indecent or grossly offensive nature,

is guilty of an offence if his purpose, or one of his purposes, in sending it is that it should, so far as falling within paragraph (a) or (b) above, cause distress or anxiety to the recipient or to any other person to whom he intends that it or its contents or nature should be communicated.

## (3) <span id="section-1-3" />

In this section references to sending include references to delivering [or transmitting][^fc803487] and to causing to be sent [, delivered or transmitted][^fc803488] and “sender” shall be construed accordingly.

## (4) <span id="section-1-4" />

### (a) <span id="section-1-4-a" />

[on conviction on indictment to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or a fine (or both);][^fkey-5a52a02dfaea8eb1f1bddd9ca3765338]

### (b) <span id="section-1-4-b" />

# 2. Northern Ireland. <span id="section-2" />

An Order in Council under paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the [^fc803490]Northern Ireland Act 1974 (legislation for Northern Ireland in the interim period) which states that it is made only for purposes corresponding to those of this Act—

## (a) <span id="section-2-a" />

shall not be subject to paragraph 1(4) and (5) of that Schedule (affirmative resolution of both Houses of Parliament); but

## (b) <span id="section-2-b" />

shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House.

# 3. Short title, commencement and extent. <span id="section-3" />

> [Extent — 3. Short title, commencement and extent.] E+W

## (1) <span id="section-3-1" />

This Act may be cited as the Malicious Communications Act 1988.

## (2) <span id="section-3-2" />

Section 1 above shall not come into force until the end of the period of two months beginning with the day on which this Act is passed.

## (3) <span id="section-3-3" />

This Act does not extend to Scotland or, except for section 2, to Northern Ireland.
