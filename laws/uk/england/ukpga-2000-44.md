# 7. Short title, construction, commencement and extent. <span id="section-7" />

## (1) <span id="section-7-1" />

This Act may be cited as the Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 2000.

## (2) <span id="section-7-2" />

For the purposes of the [^fc887396]Scotland Act 1998, this Act shall be taken to be a pre-commencement enactment within the meaning of that Act.

## (3) <span id="section-7-3" />

This Act shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order made by statutory instrument appoint; and different days may be appointed for different purposes.

## (4) <span id="section-7-4" />

This Act extends to Northern Ireland.
